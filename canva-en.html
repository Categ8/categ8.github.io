Trama Urbana

I had the opportunity to design this logo for a university project developed with my colleagues using Canva. Trama urbana is a digital publishing hous created to tell the stoires of Italian territories through their most authentic voices: those of local artisan and communities. The logo stems from a clear concept: representing the Sicilian wolrd, particulary its craftmanship. This immediately led me to the idea of majolica tiles. I chose a color palette designed to convey a sense of warmth while best representing Sicily, incorporating blue, white, and shades of orange. To capture the essence of majolica, after drawing inspiration from various ceramics, I decided to feature a floral theme. From there, I added finer details, such as the small church at the top and the delicate flower motifs.

I promessi Sposi

This is a cover I designed for a university project related to Italian literature. In this case, it was a presentation for an exam on "I promessi Sposi". I used warm, brownish tones to give the slides a 'vintage' effect, and I also utilized some Canva presets that allowed me to incorporate images of text. Here, I included pictures of pages from the book I Promessi Sposi, along with some illustrations.

Fico d'India

This is a slide from a project I created with my brother for his school. The main idea was to develop a recipe featuring a typical local ingredient, which in this case was the fico d'india. Using the images and cutouts provided by Canva I designed this slide aiming for a balanced and cohesive layout. For the color palette, I chose shades that represent the earth and the fruit itself, while to create contrast, I decided to incorporate slightly more colorful images to make the overall composition more harmonious.
