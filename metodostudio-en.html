Study Tutoring

Empathetic and personalized support to navigate university life

I'm a big believer in helping each other out and building a real community. Thanks to being so involved in student groups and my degree in Languages and Literatures - Intercultural Studies, I've really learned how to listen to people and communicate clearly. During my time at uni, I've helped a ton of classmates figure out their academic path. I also worked hands-on with international and Erasmus students while interning at the CLA
(the University Language Centre), helping them get over the language barrier.

So, I decided to put myself out there again and really help out. But how?

Through International and Language Tutoring aimed specifically at international students, making sure they settle into uni life as smoothly as possible.

But I'm not stopping there! I also want to offer hands-on help to anyone who's just started uni and feels totally lost, through university orientation and a personalized study method, because we all know there's no "one-size-fits-all" way to study. That's exactly why I want to help every single student manage their time or organize their notes to find the strategy that works best for them.

Uni can often feel like a total maze, especially when you find yourself in a new city dealing with a different language and totally new habits. My goal isn't just to help students out with their classes, but to give them the tools and the confidence they need to stand on their own two feet.
